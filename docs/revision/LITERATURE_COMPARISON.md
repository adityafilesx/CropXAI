# Literature and benchmark comparison

Primary records were inspected on 22 October 2026. This is a structured targeted audit, not a systematic review or claim that no other framework exists. Bibliography metadata and verification URLs are in `evidence/verified_sources.json`. Preprint versions are explicitly cited where journal metadata were not independently checked. Historical bibliography is preserved and uncited/unresolved keys are recorded in `evidence/reference_audit.json`. The revised bibliography is rebuilt in first-citation numerical order for the Procedia format; historical IEEE numbering remains in the archive.

| Work/year | Dataset/task | Models | Metrics/dimensions | Protocol | Limitation for this comparison | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Fairness statement.** Literature entries explain methodological relationships. Their published scores are not placed in a common leaderboard. Controlled comparisons in the revised results use the same hashed RGB test files, resizing, normalization, frozen-backbone procedure, seed list and explanation sample IDs. Architectures retain different pretraining and feature geometries; these differences limit causal architectural conclusions. The practical contribution is a tested, auditable connection between standard evaluation measures, seed-level results and manuscript assets. No new Bayesian inference algorithm, universal trust probability, or superiority over these published frameworks is asserted.
