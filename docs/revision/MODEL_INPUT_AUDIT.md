Rows follow the JPEG split by file stem, so both cohorts evaluate the identical 4,050 test items (verified by `identical_test_items` in the compliance report only within a cohort; cross-cohort equality is checked by the paired analysis).

## Sentinel-2 GeoTIFF band order (empirical)

The GeoTIFFs carry no band descriptions. The order inferred and then asserted by `src/cropxai/data.py::band_order_evidence` is:

`0:B01, 1:B02, 2:B03, 3:B04, 4:B05, 5:B06, 6:B07, 7:B08, 8:B09, 9:B10, 10:B11, 11:B12, 12:B8A`

This differs from the nominal listing (B8A before B09). Evidence on 400 deterministic patches:

* corr(B8A, B08) = 0.980 (>0.95 required) versus corr(B09, B08) = 0.753;
* mean B10 (cirrus) = 12.0 DN (<100 required); mean B11 = 1803 > mean B12 = 1102.

Under the nominal order, index 8 would have been selected as B8A; it is in fact the water-vapour band B09 (correlation 0.75 with B08). The unit test `tests/test_revision_v2.py::BandOrderEvidenceTests` shows the audit fails for a swapped file.

* Domain shift that is NOT corrected: the EuroSAT processing level is not stated in the dataset paper but appears to be Level-1C top-of-atmosphere (the cirrus band B10, absent from Level-2A products, is present with mean 12 DN), whereas Prithvi was pretrained on HLS surface reflectance; EuroSAT patches are 64x64 and are bilinearly upsampled to the 224x224 pretraining size; Sentinel-2 B8A/B11/B12 are used for HLS NIR-narrow/SWIR1/SWIR2 without band-pass or BRDF harmonization.
* Classification head: linear layer on the mean of final-layer patch tokens (CLS excluded), trained on frozen features. This differs from the segmentation/fine-tuning recipes published for Prithvi and should not be compared with them.

## Are all models on the same bands? (R3.5)

**No.** The three ImageNet-pretrained models consume RGB (3 channels); Prithvi consumes six bands from the GeoTIFF files. The two cohorts share the split and the test items but differ in input information, pretraining data and corruption units. Cross-cohort rankings are therefore reported as confounded. A controlled common-input comparison is not available: ImageNet models cannot consume the missing bands without an unvalidated input adapter, and Prithvi cannot consume RGB-only input without synthetic channels. This limitation is stated in the manuscript.
